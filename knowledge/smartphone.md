# Smartphones

A smartphone is a mobile computing device that combines
mobile telephone functionality with computing capabilities.

Modern smartphones commonly include a processor, RAM,
internal storage, camera, display, battery and wireless
communication capabilities.

Samsung, Apple and Google are examples of companies that
manufacture smartphones.
