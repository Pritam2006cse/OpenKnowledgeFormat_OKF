# Sample Story The Little Lamp

## Page1

The Little Lamp
A Short Story About Courage and Kindness
Chapter 1: A Stormy Evening
In a small village beside a wide river lived a young girl named Mira. She lived with her grandfather in
a little house at the edge of the village. Every evening, Mira lit an old brass lamp and placed it near
the window. Its warm glow helped travelers find the path home.
One autumn evening, dark clouds gathered over the village. The wind grew stronger, and rain
poured across the fields. Soon, the village lost its electricity. People closed their doors and waited
for the storm to pass.
Chapter 2: The Lost Traveler
As Mira prepared for bed, she heard a faint knock at the door. An elderly traveler stood outside,
soaked by the rain and unable to find the bridge. Mira wanted to help, but the storm frightened her.
Her grandfather handed her the brass lamp. 'Courage does not mean that you feel no fear,' he said.
'It means you choose to do what is right even when you are afraid.'
Chapter 3: A Light in the Darkness
Mira held the lamp high and walked with her grandfather along the safe village path. They guided
the traveler to the bridge, where he thanked them warmly. On their way home, Mira noticed that
neighbors had placed their own lamps in their windows. Together, the little lights made the dark
village seem bright and welcoming.
Chapter 4: The Morning After
By morning, the storm had passed. The traveler visited Mira's house to thank her again. He told the
villagers how one small lamp had helped him find safety. From that day on, the villagers agreed to
keep lights ready for anyone who might need help.
Moral of the Story
A small act of kindness can bring hope to others, and true courage is doing the right thing even
when you feel afraid.
